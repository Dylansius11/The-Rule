## Boundaries

There is no price, return, liquidity, listing or redemption promise for RULE. The current build is testnet play money; any real-value use would need legal review. RULE locks increase eligible attempts; they do not give fee discounts in this build. Each additional play still pays its normal costs.

## Considered, not delivered

A launch through a launchpad such as meme.cooking is a possible integration after the hackathon, not part of this build. There is no date, price or promise. Other roadmap ideas are considered, not commitments. See [source and collaboration](/docs/brand/source-and-collaboration).
