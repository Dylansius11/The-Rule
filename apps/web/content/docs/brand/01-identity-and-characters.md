## Ink and wax

THE RULE looks like a hand-drawn arcade on cream paper: thick ink, flat colour and a red wax seal. The frame can be playful; the ledger, hashes, amounts and verification checks stay sober, readable and labeled in words. The voice is short, deadpan and clear. Never imply a mascot knows the law or make a money claim through a character.

## Meet the cast

**Blot** is the hero, a red sealing-wax blob with a keyhole seal. Blot reacts to recorded results, never chooses a verdict. **Drip** is a pink ink-drop sidekick for progress, empty and error states; no cursor follower. **The heron clerk** keeps the public ledger and Team desk, but is not the referee and never stamps a verdict.

| State | Blot expression |
| --- | --- |
| Idle | Calm wave |
| Pending | Sweat and darting eyes, not a smile |
| YES | Jump and stars |
| NO | Tear and sympathy |
| Already tried | Shrug |
| Reveal | Gasp and glowing seal |

When text already names the state, the mascot is decorative with empty alt text. It never communicates YES or NO on its own. See [assets](/docs/brand/assets).
