## Assemble the roster

A funded player creates a team, the captain invites accounts, and invitees join. A team has at most five members. The first funded participation snapshots the roster and locks it through the round; a fully refunded provisional first play can release an otherwise unused snapshot.

## One team, one line

Members fund the vault, which pays team play fees, storage and guess stakes. Each signer still needs wallet NEAR for transaction gas. A team shares one previous-YES line, one pending play and one attempt budget. Its base is 50 attempts, with eligible member locks adding attempts up to the 250 cap. Each extra play still costs the normal fee.

Winner and contributor rewards split equally among the stored round roster, with rounding dust rolling over. Vault principal is different: after terminal settlement, each depositor withdraws their pro-rata remaining vault position to personal credit, then may withdraw credit to their wallet. Historical members can claim their stored round entitlement even after leaving; keep the original team and round IDs. The Team flow was exercised in a three-member testnet rehearsal.
