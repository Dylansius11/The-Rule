# THE RULE: design

Status: direction D "Hand-Inked" chosen by the founder on 2026-11-08 (closes B1). Stack D2 confirmed the same day. This file is the binding visual and interaction contract for `apps/web`. PRD fixes what each screen does; this file fixes how it looks, moves and reads.

## TL;DR

- **Feel:** a hand-drawn arcade on cream paper. Thick wobbly ink, flat loud fills, a red wax blob called **Blot** who lives on the page and reacts to real results.
- **Rule of thumb:** the fun lives in the frame; the data stays sober. Ledger rows, hashes, amounts and verify checks are plain, high-contrast and labeled in words.
- **Honesty is part of the design:** a pending request never looks like a verdict, the mascot never knows the rule, every money state says whether funds moved, and the deployment label is always visible.
- **Reference:** thewhoopsies.co.uk (founder recording, local `asset/reference.mp4`). We borrow its attitude (drawn UI, character in situations, scroll theatre, deadpan copy), not its palette, characters or layouts.

## Type

All fonts are self-hosted through Fontsource (no third-party font request).

| Role | Family | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Londrina Solid 900/400 | headlines, buttons, stamps, reel digits, nav. UPPERCASE. Poster treatment for heroes: fill + ink stroke + offset shadow |
| Annotation | Delicious Handrawn | asides, speech bubbles, arrow notes. Tilted -1° to -6°. Never for anything the player must act on |
| Body and UI | Nunito Variable | explanations, labels, form text. 17 px minimum on mobile, 18-22 px on desktop |
| Data | JetBrains Mono Variable | hashes, amounts, log rows, receipts, times. Tabular numerals. 14 px minimum, hashes 12 px minimum |

## Ink

- Outlines are 3.5 px ink (small parts 3 px, art already carries its own line). Corners use asymmetric radii so no two corners match.
- The wobble is an SVG displacement filter applied to a **pseudo-layer behind the content**, so text and inputs stay sharp. Use it on at most about 30 surfaces per view (cards, buttons, stickers, bands). Tables, list rows and inputs use plain straight borders: they must stay crisp and cheap at 10k rows.
- Buttons are slabs on a visible lip: the face drops 6-7 px onto the lip when pressed. No blurred shadows anywhere; depth is offset ink.
- Section edges are hand-drawn waves. On `ink` sections, art sits on a paper sticker or gets a paper halo (`drop-shadow` rings), because the art's own outline is black.

## Characters

| Character | Role | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blot** | Hero. A blob of red sealing wax with a keyhole seal on its belly | Reacts to recorded results only. Never "knows" the rule, never chooses YES/NO, never talks about money or price. On the landing hero he is a toon-shaded 3D model built in code from the master drawing (`src/mascot/Blot3D.tsx`): flat brand inks, boiling ink outlines, eyes and body follow the pointer, breathes, blinks, waves, squashes and spins his seal when poked. Reduced motion, missing WebGL or a lost context show the 2D `blot/hero` art in the same box |
| **Drip** | Pink ink-drop sidekick | Lives in progress and empty states (crawls along progress bars, peeks over cards, sleeps in empty results, startles on errors). **No cursor follower** (founder, 2026-11-08) |
| **The heron clerk** | Cameo: keeps the public ledger and the Team desk | Never presented as the referee and never stamps a player's verdict |

Expression mapping for the play desk: `idle` (calm wave), `pending` (sweat, darting eyes, never smiling), `yes` (jump, stars), `no` (tear, sympathetic), `already` (shrug, "seen it"), `reveal` (gasp, glowing seal). The mascot image is decoration (`alt=""`) whenever the same state is already written in text next to it.

## Layout

The landing and the game are separate (founder, 2026-11-08). The landing sells and explains; the game is a round room. Entering the game needs no wallet ("soft gate"): reading the ledger and verifying are open to everyone, and the wallet is asked for only at an action.

- **Landing header (sticky):** Blot peeking over the bottom rule, the wordmark **THE RULE**, anchor links (How to play, The last law, Season) and the primary "Enter the game" button. No wallet chip. On mobile a sticky "Enter the game" bar appears once the hero scrolls away.
- **App header (sticky):** peek + wordmark (back to the landing), Play, Season, Account, a `?` that reopens How to play, a settings gear, then the wallet chip (credit and $RULE with the seal mark) or Connect. Inside the round room a sticky sub-bar carries the Solo/Team switch and the tabs Stamp, Ledger, Guess, Verify.
- **Deployment strip:** under every header, the label from `deploymentLabel(active.kind)` plus a Live/Demo toggle. Without a configured demo instance, Demo opens an honest "not deployed yet" dialog; with one, it switches and reloads. In Demo Mode the strip is a permanent tomato tape reading "Demo Mode - preselected rule" on every page.
- **Mobile (390 px):** in the app, a bottom tab bar (Stamp, Ledger, Guess, Verify, More) with More holding Season, Account, Team crew, How to play, Settings and Demo. Tap targets 44 px minimum. No horizontal scroll at any width. Wide screens use a fluid container up to 1440 px with full-bleed bands and doodles in the gutters.
- **Footer:** the full footer (trust sentence, replay limit, testnet and no-price disclaimer, accounts, Blot in the pool) lives on the landing. The app has one slim line whose "Trust model" opens a sheet with the same sentences; Verify keeps the replay limit beside its results.
- **Settings (gear):** sound on/off and volume, music on/off and volume (default on at 0.3, starts after the first tap), reduce motion (for people who get dizzy from animation; the OS setting always applies too), show How to play.
- **How to play:** a five-step illustrated modal that opens on the first visit to `/play` and from `?`: pick a number and a word; the sealed law stamps YES or NO (YES mints $RULE, fees fund the pot); read the ledger and spot the pattern; commit a guess with a stake (earliest correct commit wins); the rule opens and your browser replays every verdict.

## The sealed-law scroll story (landing)

A pinned sequence about 400 vh tall that must land somewhere:

1. A giant drawn eye (SVG, iris follows the pointer) and "WHAT'S INSIDE?".
2. Scroll zooms into the pupil until the screen is ink.
3. In the dark, star doodles drift in parallax and the sealed envelope floats in, turning.
4. The wax seal grows over the envelope while three lines appear one by one: "One law. Sealed before the first play." "It stamps every play YES or NO." "It opens only at the reveal."
5. Blot slides in covering its eyes: "Not even Blot peeks."
6. The pin releases into **"The last law, opened"**: the latest revealed round from chain (rule in English, plays, winner) and a button to replay every verdict in Verify.

Reduced motion: the same content as a static, readable stack.

## Motion

Feel comes from five to eight small things in the same 100 ms, then rest.

| Event | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| Press STAMP | face drops onto the lip (90 ms), desk thumps 5 px, button disables |
| Pending | Blot pending art wobbles; three dots; the text says what is real ("Waiting for the sealed program. Your fee is safe.") |
| YES | stamp slams (scale 2.6 → 1, -8°), star sticker slaps the slip, 9 doodle stars burst, Blot hops with a squashing shadow, $RULE counts up, the log row flashes sun |
| NO | smaller slam, Blot squishes, one tear, no shake |
| Already tried | slip swaps text, shrug art, nothing else moves |
| Reel digit | rolls from above or below (300 ms) |
| Display letters | lift and tilt toward the pointer (250 ms spring) |
| Marquee | glyphs travel along the wave, paused off screen |
| Reveal | the seal cracks, the rule types out (38 ms per character), checks go spinner → tick |

Curves: `--spring: cubic-bezier(.34,1.7,.5,1)` for pops, `--out: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)` for settles. `prefers-reduced-motion` (and an in-app toggle) collapses every row to an instant state change.

## Sound

Short, dry, tactile SFX from a hand-inked paper world (rubber stamps, wax, paper, a rubbery mascot), generated once with ElevenLabs by `scripts/web/gen-sfx.mjs` and shipped as small MP3s under `public/sfx/` (about 154 KB total, 25 sounds including nav, sign, stamp-roll, win and correct; Blot's squeak is a faster, lower, male cartoon vocalization, 2026-11-12). Sounds answer only a user action or the user's own result (stamp, verdict, replay, poke, modal, tab); never page load, scroll or polling. Poking the 3D hero Blot plays a springy `jump` as he hops; the 2D fallback keeps the `blot` squeak because it does not hop. On by default at a moderate volume, muted or adjusted in Settings, silent until the first gesture.

**Music (founder, 2026-11-09; theme changed to playful detective 2026-11-11):** one quiet playful-detective-caper bed (sneaking upright bass, pizzicato strings, vibraphone, muted trumpet, brushed drums; `public/music/ambient.mp3`, 30 s, about -17.6 LUFS, 128 kbps stereo; since 2026-11-12 the original loop is kept as the base and a cheerful marimba, pluck, glockenspiel and clap layer is mixed on its measured beat grid (about 112 BPM) by `scripts/web/gen-music.mjs`; the Music API needs a paid plan and refused this key; not audited by ear). On by default but it can only start after the first tap, loads lazily, loops with a two-second crossfade, dips to under half while any effect plays, pauses when the tab is hidden, and has its own switch and volume in Settings (default 0.3). It never changes meaning: it carries no information about verdicts.

## Voice

Deadpan, short, never about money. Say: "Pick anything. The law stamps it." "Already tried that one. Free this time." "Rejected. The fee joined the pot." Never say: provably fair, unhackable, nobody can ever know, guaranteed, moon, earn, Blot knows the rule. The only trust sentence is in `TRUST_SENTENCE`. No price, return or liquidity promise for $RULE.

## Accessibility and performance

- Keyboard: every control reachable, visible 4 px focus ring, reels operable with arrow keys, word chips as a radio group, skip link.
- Live regions announce play results and replay outcomes in words.
- 390 px and 1280 px layouts, no horizontal overflow, 44 px tap targets.
- Images carry intrinsic size from the manifest (no layout shift), lazy below the fold; hero and idle expression preload.
- The ink filter is limited as above; long lists never use it.
