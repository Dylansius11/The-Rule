## The game in one screen

THE RULE is a testnet game of experiments and deduction. Each round has a sealed law. Pick a number and a word, stamp your play, and the referee returns YES or NO. Read the public ledger, propose a law, and commit and reveal your guess. The earliest correct commit wins the winner's share of the pot. Once the round opens, replay its verdicts against the revealed law.

## Secret and public

During investigation, the law and its salt are sealed; the commitment, round state, plays and settled verdicts are public. A play also depends on recorded time and your line: your previous YES in that round. The commitment binds the eventual opening; it is not the law itself.

Humans can spot patterns together. Agents can read the same ledger, enumerate the public grammar and submit through the same contract. Both face the same semantic question: does a candidate match the law over the entire legal domain, not just the plays seen so far?
