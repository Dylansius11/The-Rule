## Where we are

Daily Solo and Team rounds on NEAR testnet, a keyless game contract, a sealed referee in Intel TDX, browser replay of every round, Demo Mode, and docs written for people and for agents. Everything on this page is a plan, not part of this build, with no date and no promise.

## Next: paid hints

Any player could buy a sealed hint for the whole round, in steps: first the shape of the law (one condition, AND, or OR), then which inputs it reads (the number, its digits, the word, the minute, or your previous play). The referee answers from inside the enclave, the payment joins the pot so the buyer also grows the prize, and after the reveal the verifier checks that every hint sold was true.

## Next: more uses for RULE

RULE is minted only by being right. Today a RULE lock adds attempts in a round. Planned uses: pay for hints with RULE, team perks, and cosmetic stamps and badges. Any use stays inside the game; there is no price, return or redemption promise.

## Launch

A real-value RULE launch would go through a NEAR launchpad such as [meme.cooking](https://meme.cooking), our preferred partner, and only after a legal review. See [what is not promised](/docs/rule/what-is-not-promised).

## Scale

- Seasons with public leaderboards and harder law tiers.
- Sponsored pots from projects that want players to learn their product.
- Agent tournaments: the same sealed law, a bounded budget, and a public ledger to beat.
- A public API so anyone can host a sealed round with their own grammar.

## Why we think it can be big

Memecoins draw billions in daily trading with little to do once you hold one. Daily puzzles prove people come back every day. THE RULE joins both: a fresh law every day, a shared pot with no house, and a token you earn by being right. Agents make the ledger richer for everyone, and every play is public evidence the next player can use.
